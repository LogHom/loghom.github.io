LogHom Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

The short version: your home's information belongs to you. It lives on your devices and syncs privately through your own iCloud account. LogHom has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. We do not collect, sell, or share your data, because we never see it.

What LogHom stores, and where

Everything you put into LogHom (properties, items, photos, scanned documents, notes, reminders, contacts, and vehicle details) is stored in a database on your device and synced to your private iCloud database, so it stays up to date on your other devices signed into the same Apple Account. It is protected by your device's built-in encryption, by your Apple Account, and by Face ID if you enable App Lock. LogHom cannot access your iCloud data; only your devices can.

On-device intelligence

LogHom's smart features run entirely on your iPhone using Apple's frameworks:

  • Text recognition (reading model plates and scanned documents) uses Apple's Vision framework, on-device.
  • Ask LogHom uses Apple Intelligence's on-device language model. Your questions and records are never sent to a server.
  • Voice notes are transcribed only on your iPhone using Apple's speech recognition. Your voice is never sent to a server. If on-device transcription isn't available for your language, voice notes are turned off instead.

What leaves your device

  • iCloud sync: your records are stored in your private iCloud database, operated by Apple and tied to your Apple Account. LogHom has no access to it. You can turn off iCloud for LogHom in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Weather: each time the Properties screen shows current conditions, each property's latitude and longitude are sent to Apple's WeatherKit service. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather.
  • Address lookup: when you save a property address, the address is sent to Apple Maps to find its coordinates. If you choose to fill in an address from your current location, that location is sent to Apple to look up the street address.
  • The LogHom app saves each property's address and coordinates with your other records, on your device and in your private iCloud data. We, LogHom's developer, never receive these requests or their results, and have no servers to keep them on. Apple handles weather, map, and location requests under its own terms; see the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Subscriptions: LogHom Pro is purchased through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; LogHom never receives your payment details or Apple Account information.
  • Exports, backups, and shares you initiate: when you export a Property Packet PDF or a backup file, or share content through the iOS share sheet, that data goes only where you send it.
  • Support email: if you email us, we receive what you send: your email address, your message, and the app version LogHom fills in for you. We use it only to reply.
  • Calendar and notifications: if you enable them, reminders are written to your device's calendar and notification system. They stay in your Apple ecosystem under Apple's privacy terms.

Permissions LogHom asks for

Camera (scanning documents and model plates), Photo Library (attaching photos), Microphone and Speech Recognition (voice notes), Location (filling in a property address), Face ID (App Lock), Calendar (reminder sync), and Notifications (reminders). Each is optional and requested only when you first use the feature.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes LogHom's data from that device only. Your records stay in your private iCloud data and return if you reinstall LogHom while signed into the same Apple Account. Inside LogHom, Me, then Settings, then Delete All Data deletes your records. Backup files you exported are separate and stay wherever you saved them. If you'd like help making sure your data is removed from iCloud, email support@loghom.com.

Analytics and tracking

None. LogHom contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Children

LogHom is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
