Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, in a database protected by your device's encryption, and synced privately through your own iCloud account so your other devices stay up to date. LogHom has no servers and no account system; we never see your data. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

How do I back up my data?

In LogHom, open Me, then Settings, and tap Back Up Now to create a complete backup file, which you can store in Files, iCloud Drive, or anywhere you like. Restore from Backup on the same screen brings it back. Restoring replaces everything in LogHom. Because LogHom syncs through iCloud, it can also replace what your other devices show. Before restoring, LogHom saves a copy of that iPhone's current data, which you can find under Saved Before Restore in Settings. Your device's normal iPhone backup also includes LogHom's data.

Why don't I see Ask LogHom?

Ask LogHom uses Apple Intelligence's on-device language model, so it appears only on iPhones that support Apple Intelligence. Everything else, from capture to search to reminders, works on all supported devices.

Why can't I record a voice note?

LogHom transcribes voice notes only on your iPhone and never sends your voice to a server. If on-device transcription isn't available for your iPhone's language, LogHom explains that and lets you type the note instead.

The camera didn't read a model plate correctly.

Plate reading works best with even lighting and the plate filling most of the frame. Worn or engraved plates can be tricky, and you can always correct any field before saving. If a clear plate consistently misreads, email us a photo of it (nothing is shared unless you choose to send it) and we'll improve recognition.

Does LogHom work on iPad or Mac?

LogHom is designed for iPhone today. Other platforms are on the list. Tell us which you'd use and it helps us prioritize.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes LogHom's data from that device only. Your records stay in your private iCloud data and come back if you reinstall. Inside LogHom, Me, then Settings, then Delete All Data deletes your records, and you can remove individual properties, items, or documents at any time. If you'd like help making sure your data is removed from iCloud, email support@loghom.com.
